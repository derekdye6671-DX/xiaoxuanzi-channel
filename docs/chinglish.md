## 一、10 个值得优先改掉的中式英语表达

| 中式说法（历史记录） | 更地道的说法 | 例句（新编练习） |
|---|---|---|
| 1. **share me the report** | **share the report with me**（把报告分享给我） | Could you **share the latest sales report with me** before lunch? |
| 2. **I will reply you.** | **I'll reply to you.**／**I'll get back to you.**（后者适合“核实后再回复”） | I'll check the storage capacity and **get back to you** this afternoon. |
| 3. **I want AI are accuracy.** | **I want AI to be accurate.**／**I care about the accuracy of AI answers.** | I don't need an AI tool to sound confident; **I want it to be accurate**. |
| 4. **The business is more and more tough.** | **Business has been getting tougher lately.**（近来生意越来越难做） | **Business has been getting tougher lately**, especially when customers are cutting costs. |
| 5. **so many spare time** | **a lot of spare time**（大量空闲时间）；**some downtime**（工作间隙的空档） | I sometimes get **some downtime** during business trips to experiment with AI. |
| 6. **add my sleeping time one hour more** | **get an extra hour of sleep**（多睡一个小时） | If I had more time, I'd **get an extra hour of sleep** every night. |
| 7. **fix my physical exercise** | **get back into a regular workout routine**（重新恢复规律锻炼） | I'm trying to **get back into a regular workout routine** before work. |
| 8. **the science fiction of Asimov** | **Asimov's science fiction**／**Isaac Asimov's work** | I've always been fascinated by **Asimov's science fiction**, especially his ideas about robots. |
| 9. **learn many new abilities** | **pick up new skills**（学会一些新技能） | If AI could handle my routine work, I'd have more time to **pick up new skills**. |
| 10. **the most important spirit is not admire but doubt** | **Science is driven not by blind admiration, but by critical inquiry.** | Good scientists challenge established theories; **science is driven by critical inquiry**. |

### 这 10 条背后的三种规律

1. **动词搭配按中文直译。** 英语说 `share something **with** someone`、`reply **to** someone`；不能总套用中文“分享给我、回复我”的顺序。
2. **把中文抽象名词逐字拼成英文。** “增加睡眠时间”在口语里通常说 `get more sleep`；“恢复锻炼”直接说 `get back into a workout routine`。
3. **单词大致认识，但习惯组合不自然。** `pick up new skills`、`downtime`、`critical inquiry` 往往比逐词翻译更像母语者的表达。

**易混辨析：** `spare time` 是可用于自由安排的时间；`downtime` 更偏工作、活动之间的空档。`I confused them` 可以表示“我把他们弄糊涂了”，如果本意是“我把两个概念搞混了”，通常说 **I got them mixed up**。

## 二、10 个有历史纠错记录的语法错误

这里单独保留语法规则；同一句有时既含中式搭配，也含语法问题。考虑到你自己明确说过**条件句、虚拟语气是长期难点**，十条里特意多放了这类实际出错过的句型。

| # | 既往错误句／片段 | 正确表达 | 核心规则及可直接套用的例句 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **I suggest you to spend more time...** | **I suggest spending more time...**／**I suggest that you spend more time...** | `suggest + -ing` 或 `suggest (that) + 主语 + 动词原形`。**I suggest that you try playing baseball.** 不用 `suggest somebody to do`。 |
| 2 | **I'll consider to develop...** | **I'd consider developing...** | `consider + -ing`，不是 `consider to do`。**I'd consider developing a small AI product.** |
| 3 | **If my client cancel the meeting...** | **If my client cancels the meeting, I'll...** | **第一类条件句**：可能真实发生的未来情况，`if + 一般现在时`；第三人称单数加 `-s`。**If my client cancels the meeting, I'll work on my AI project.** |
| 4 | **If I'm a software engineer, I'll...**（当时意为“假如我现在是工程师”） | **If I were a software engineer, I'd...** | **第二类条件句**：与目前事实不同或设想遥远，`If I were... , I would...`。**If I were a software engineer, I'd build my own tools.** |
| 5 | **If I were 20, I will...** | **If I were 20, I would...** | 第二类条件句从句已经用 `were`，主句一般用 `would/could/might`，别突然跳回 `will`。**If I were 20 again, I'd probably take more risks.** |
| 6 | **If the money weren't be issue...** | **If money weren't an issue...** | `were` 本身就是系动词，不再叠加 `be`；可数名词 `issue` 用 `an`。**If money weren't an issue, I'd travel around the world.** |
| 7 | **If I hadn't discovered baseball, I would play badminton (then).** | **If I hadn't discovered baseball, I would have played badminton (then).** | **第三类条件句**：假设改变过去，结果也发生在过去，`If + had + 过去分词, would/might/could have + 过去分词`。**If I hadn't joined the team, I might not have met those friends.** |
| 8 | **If I hadn't discovered baseball, my experiences will be different.** | **If I hadn't discovered baseball, my experiences would be different today.** | **混合条件句**：假设过去不同，结果落在**现在**，用 `If + had done, would + 动词原形`。**If I hadn't taken up baseball, my life would be different today.** |
| 9 | **Our products are more better...** | **Our products are much better...** | `better` 已是比较级，不能再加 `more`。用 `much/far/a lot` 加强。比较对象应对齐：**Our products are much better than our competitors' products.** |
| 10 | **after I back to China** | **after I returned to China**／**after I got back to China** | `back` 不能直接充当这句的谓语动词；叙述过去经历用过去式。**Protests broke out shortly after I returned to China.** |

### 条件句：你只需先问自己“结果发生在什么时候？”

| 类型 | 你想表达的真实意思 | 句型 | 贴近你经历的示例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First** | 明天真有可能发生 | `If + 现在时, will + 动词原形` | **If I have time tomorrow, I'll practice English.** |
| **Second** | 假设“现在／未来是另一种情况” | `If + 过去式 / were, would + 动词原形` | **If I were the Red Sox manager, I'd focus on young players.** |
| **Third** | 假设“过去做过另一件事”，结果也在过去 | `If + had done, would have done` | **If I hadn't discovered baseball, I might not have met those friends.** |
| **Mixed** | 假设改变过去，结果影响**今天** | `If + had done, would + 动词原形` | **If I hadn't discovered baseball, my life would be different today.** |

**两个很重要的细节：**

- `If I were...` 不意味着“不可能发生”。它只是表明你把某件事暂时放进**想象情景**。例如“如果我是女人”“如果我是红袜主教练”，都适合 `If I were...`。
- `would have done` 是对**过去结果的假设**；`would do / would be` 常用于**现在或未来的假设结果**。不能只看到句首 `If I hadn't...`，就自动认定主句必须是 `would have...`，还得看结果的时间。

### 一个明确的进步：suggest 已经开始用对了

旧记录（Day 10）：`I suggest you to spend more time...` → 当时需要纠正为 `I suggest spending... / I suggest that you spend...`。

最新检索的 Day 21 反馈：你已经能正确使用 **`I suggest that you play baseball.`** 这恰恰说明旧错误并非永久弱点。当天练习还涉及 `rules are`（复数主谓一致）、`joining / actually playing` 作主语，以及 `relieve stress / blow off steam`（缓解压力）。由于检索到的是纠错摘要而非整段原文，这些**没有被伪装成你当时逐字说错的句子**。

## 四、下次复习的优先次序

**第一优先：** `suggest / consider + -ing` 与 `suggest that + 主语 + 原形`。在真实聊天里，用一两句就能反复练到。  
**第二优先：** First／Second／Third／Mixed 条件句的时间逻辑，尤其 `will → would → would have` 的选择。  
**第三优先：** 固定搭配（`get back to you`, `pick up skills`, `get an extra hour of sleep`）。每次只选 3 条，把它们用到你今天真实发生的事上。

**判断是否学会的标准：** 不是看到正确答案后觉得“原来如此”，而是**隔 24 小时仍能不用提示说对，并能换一个生活情境再说对一次**。
